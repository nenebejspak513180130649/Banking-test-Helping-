Study smarter

A simple preparation roadmap

Build strong basics first, then practise under time pressure.

01

Quantitative aptitude

Practise percentages, ratios, averages, profit and loss, number series, rounding, and word problems. Write down the method for every mistake.

02

General knowledge

Revise Pakistan studies, current affairs, geography, basic banking terms, financial inclusion, and everyday computer knowledge.

03

Personality assessment

Answer honestly and consistently. There is no universally correct personality profile; suggested answers in a practice file are not guaranteed official answers.

04

Banking fundamentals

Learn deposits, withdrawals, account types, KYC, customer service, digital banking, cheque basics, and safe handling of customer information.

05

Timed practice

Attempt short timed quizzes, review wrong answers, and repeat weak topics instead of only memorising answer letters.

06

Check the source

Always check the latest official recruitment announcement, eligibility criteria, syllabus, test format, and deadlines before applying.
